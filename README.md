## Best Practices for SMS Campaigns

Effective messaging starts with a clear purpose and a relevant audience. Keep messages concise, identify your business where appropriate, and provide a clear next step when needed.

For promotional messages, obtain the required consent, respect opt-out requests, and follow applicable Saudi telecommunications and data-protection requirements.

For transactional and OTP messages, use appropriate security controls, limit verification attempts, and avoid including unnecessary sensitive information. Monitor delivery information and investigate recurring errors to improve reliability.
